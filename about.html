ABOUT US

About Studora

Studora provides free online calculators designed to make everyday academic calculations simpler for school and college students.

Our Mission

Academic calculations should be straightforward. Studora aims to help students calculate their grades, check attendance, and understand their academic results without unnecessary complexity.

Our goal is to make these tools easy to use, accessible on different devices, and available without a paid subscription.

Our Tools

Studora offers calculators for common academic tasks. Choose a tool below to get started.

CGPA Calculator Calculate your cumulative grade point average using your academic results. SGPA Calculator Calculate your semester grade point average based on your subjects and credits. Attendance Calculator Check your attendance percentage and estimate the classes you need to attend. Percentage Calculator Convert marks obtained into a percentage using your total marks.

Who Is Studora For?

Studora is intended for school students, college students, and anyone who needs help with basic academic calculations.

Whether you are checking your semester grades or planning your attendance, the tools aim to make the calculation process quicker and easier.

Our Approach

We aim to keep our calculators simple and provide explanations that help students understand their results.

Calculations and academic requirements can vary by institution. Always check your college's official grading system and attendance rules before making important academic decisions.

About This Website

Studora is an independent student-tools website. It is not an official college or university service, and it is not affiliated with any educational institution.

For information about how the website handles data, please read our Privacy Policy. If you have a question or feedback, visit our Contact page.
