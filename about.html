About Studora

Welcome to Studora, a platform created to make everyday academic calculations easier for students.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide simple, accessible and free online tools that help students save time and focus on learning.

Our Tools

Studora currently provides:

  • SGPA Calculator
  • CGPA Calculator
  • Attendance Calculator
  • Percentage Calculator

Who Is Studora For?

Studora is designed for school and college students who need quick academic calculations.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep our tools easy to use, accessible on different devices, and useful for students. Please verify important academic calculations against your institution's official rules.

Studora is an independent student-tools website.
