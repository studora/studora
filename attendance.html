How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage shows how many classes you attended out of the total classes conducted. You can use it to check whether you meet your college's minimum attendance requirement.

Attendance Percentage Formula

Attendance (%) = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Conducted) × 100

Example of Attendance Calculation

Suppose your college has conducted 40 classes and you attended 30. Your attendance percentage is:

(30 ÷ 40) × 100 = 75%

If your target is 75%, you currently meet that target. Enter your own class counts above to calculate your attendance and plan for upcoming classes.

How Many Classes Do You Need to Attend?

If your attendance is below your target, the calculator estimates how many consecutive classes you must attend without missing any to reach the required percentage. The estimate assumes no additional absences.

How Many Classes Can You Miss?

If you already meet your target, the calculator estimates the maximum number of additional classes you could miss while remaining at or above that percentage. Your institution may apply separate attendance rules, so always check its official policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my attendance percentage?

Divide the number of classes you attended by the total classes conducted, then multiply the result by 100.

How many classes do I need to attend for 75% attendance?

It depends on your current attendance. Enter your total classes, attended classes, and a target of 75% in the calculator to estimate how many consecutive classes you need to attend.

Can I reach 100% attendance after missing a class?

No. If you have already missed a class, attending additional classes can increase your percentage but cannot bring it back to exactly 100%.

Is 75% attendance compulsory in every college?

No. Requirements vary by institution, course, and applicable academic rules. Check your college's official attendance policy to confirm the minimum requirement.
