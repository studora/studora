How CGPA is calculated

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It shows your overall academic performance across all the semesters you have completed. This calculator uses the credit-weighted method: each semester's SGPA counts in proportion to that semester's total credits, so a semester with more credits has a bigger effect on your CGPA.

CGPA = (SGPA₁ × Credits₁ + SGPA₂ × Credits₂ + …) ÷ (Credits₁ + Credits₂ + …)

The calculator accepts SGPA values from 0 to 10, which is the 10-point scale used by many universities in India. Always check your university's rules, because grading schemes differ.

Worked example

Suppose you have finished two semesters. These are the same values the calculator loads by default, so you can click "Calculate CGPA" and compare.

Semester SGPA Total credits SGPA × credits
Semester 1 8.0 20 160
Semester 2 7.5 22 165
Total 42 325

CGPA = 325 ÷ 42 = 7.74 (rounded to two decimal places).

A simple average of the two SGPAs would give 7.75. The gap is small here because the credits are similar. It grows when your semesters have very different credit totals.

Frequently asked questions

Is CGPA just the average of my SGPAs?

Not always. Many universities weight each SGPA by credits, as this calculator does. If every semester has the same credits, both methods give the same result. If your university uses a different rule, follow your university's official method.

Can I use this for a 4.0 grading scale?

No. This calculator only accepts SGPA values from 0 to 10. If your university uses a 4.0 scale, the result here will not match your official CGPA.

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

There is no single rule that every university follows. Some publish a multiplier and others use their own formula. Check your university's official conversion notice before using a converted figure anywhere important.

Does this page save or send my marks anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, and the values you type are not sent anywhere by this page.
