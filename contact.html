Contact Studora

Have a suggestion, discovered a calculation issue, or want to recommend a new student tool? This page explains how to prepare useful feedback for Studora.

Feedback and Suggestions

Your feedback can help identify improvements for our academic calculators and future tools. Suggestions for making the website easier to use are also welcome.

Report a Problem

If a calculator gives an unexpected result, please include the following information when reporting the issue:

  • The name of the calculator.
  • The steps you followed.
  • The sample values you entered.
  • The result you expected and the result displayed.
  • Your browser and device, if relevant.

Use fictional or non-sensitive sample values. Never include passwords, financial account details, or private academic records.

Contact Availability

Studora does not currently provide a direct contact method on this page.

We do not want to display an unverified email address or a contact form that cannot deliver messages.
