How to calculate percentage of marks

A percentage tells you how many marks you obtained compared with the total marks available. Students commonly use percentages to understand their exam performance, calculate test scores, and compare results.

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Enter your marks in the calculator above. The calculator divides your obtained marks by the total marks and multiplies the result by 100.

Percentage calculation example

Suppose you scored 420 marks out of a maximum of 500. You can calculate your percentage as follows:

Marks obtained: 420

Total marks: 500

Calculation: (420 ÷ 500) × 100

Final percentage: 84%

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my exam percentage?

Divide the marks you obtained by the total possible marks and multiply the result by 100. For example, 360 out of 400 is (360 ÷ 400) × 100 = 90%.

Can I calculate percentage for any exam?

Yes. You can use this calculator for school exams, college tests, university examinations, and other assessments, provided you know your obtained marks and the total possible marks.

What happens if I score zero marks?

If you obtain zero marks out of a positive total, your percentage is 0%.

Can I enter decimal marks?

Yes. The calculator accepts decimal values, which can be useful when your assessment uses fractional marks.

Is percentage the same as CGPA?

No. Percentage represents marks relative to the maximum possible marks, while CGPA represents cumulative grade-point performance. The conversion between CGPA and percentage depends on your university's official rules.
