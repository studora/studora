YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Studora. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use our website and academic calculators.

Studora provides online tools intended to help students with common academic calculations. Please read this policy to understand the privacy considerations associated with using the website.

2. Information You Enter

Studora's academic calculators are designed to perform calculations directly in your browser. For these calculations, the values you enter are processed by the calculator's JavaScript code to produce results.

The calculator code itself does not need to transmit those entered values to a Studora server to perform its calculations. However, your browser, hosting provider, or any additional third-party services may process technical information according to their applicable practices.

Do not enter passwords, financial account information, or other sensitive personal information into the calculators.

3. Technical Information and Hosting

When you visit a website, technical information may be processed by the browser, hosting infrastructure, or related services. Depending on the service and its configuration, this information may include your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and request timestamps.

Studora is hosted using GitHub Pages. Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's own data practices.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by third-party services integrated into a website. Their use depends on which services are active and how they are configured.

We do not describe any particular analytics or advertising service as active unless it has actually been implemented.

If Studora introduces analytics, advertising, or other services that materially affect privacy, this policy should be updated to describe the relevant practices.

5. Third-Party Websites and Services

Studora may link to external websites or services for convenience. These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Visiting an external website is subject to that website's terms and privacy policy. Studora does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

6. Data Security

We aim to keep Studora useful and accessible. However, no website, internet connection, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the website.

7. Children's Privacy

Studora provides general educational tools for students. The website is not designed to request sensitive personal information from children.

If you believe personal information has been submitted through a feature on Studora and you have a concern about its handling, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

8. Your Choices

You can choose not to enter information into the calculators. You can also review your browser's privacy settings to manage cookies and other website data.

Blocking certain website data or scripts may affect how some website features work.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Studora's features, hosting arrangements, or third-party integrations change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date when appropriate.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page for available contact information.

This page provides general information about website privacy practices. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Review and adapt this policy to reflect the website's actual services, applicable laws, and data practices.
