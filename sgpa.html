How SGPA is calculated

SGPA stands for Semester Grade Point Average. It summarizes your performance in a single semester. Each subject's grade points are weighted by that subject's credits, so a 4-credit subject affects your SGPA more than a 2-credit subject.

SGPA = (Credits₁ × Grade points₁ + Credits₂ × Grade points₂ + …) ÷ (Credits₁ + Credits₂ + …)

This calculator accepts grade points from 0 to 10, which is the 10-point scale used by many universities in India. Your university decides how each letter grade or mark range maps to grade points, so use the values from your own grade sheet.

Worked example

SGPA = 113 ÷ 14 = 8.07 (rounded to two decimal places).

Frequently asked questions

What are grade points?

Grade points are the numbers your university assigns to each letter grade or mark range, such as a grade point value for an A or a B. The mapping differs between universities, so check your marksheet or grading policy instead of assuming.

What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?

SGPA covers one semester. CGPA combines all your completed semesters. Once you have your SGPA for each semester, you can use the CGPA Calculator to combine them.

Which subjects should I include?

Add every subject your university counts toward SGPA. Rules for non-credit courses, audit courses and failed subjects differ between universities, so follow your university's official rules for what to include.

Can I use this for a 4.0 grading scale?

No. Grade points here must be between 0 and 10. If your university uses a 4.0 scale, the result will not match your official SGPA.
